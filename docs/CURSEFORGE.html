ItemLockForever stops you from selling, destroying or disenchanting the items you want to keep. Alt+Click an item to lock it, Alt+Click it again to unlock it. That's it.

Made for World of Warcraft: Forever. There's nothing to set up, and it works with the default bags and with bag addons built on Blizzard's item buttons.

Features

  • Alt+Click to lock — in your bags, the bank or the character window. A padlock marks locked items, and their tooltip says they're locked. You can pick another click, or use a key binding instead.
  • Can't be sold — at a vendor, locked items are held with the game's own item lock (they show greyed out), so right-clicking or dragging them does nothing. If something still sells one, like the Sell All Junk button, it's bought back straight away.
  • Can't be destroyed — dropping a locked item outside your bags puts it back in its slot instead of asking to destroy it.
  • Can't be disenchanted — while Disenchant waits for you to pick an item, locked items are held too, so clicking one does nothing. Enchants, poisons and other spells still work on locked items.
  • Tells you why — trying any of that on a locked item shows red error text and plays an error sound you can choose (or turn off).
  • Gear is locked as that exact item — a second copy of the same sword stays sellable, and the lock follows the item if you mail it to an alt.
  • Stacks are locked one at a time — locked items keep their lock when stacks merge or split, and when you sort your bags.
  • Every stack at once — in EllesmereUI Bags' All Items view, where one button stands for all your stacks of an item, Alt+Click locks or unlocks every stack, including stacks you pick up later.
  • Hold to unlock (optional) — unlocking takes holding the click for a second, so a stray click can't unlock anything.

Commands and options

CommandWhat it does
/ilfOpen the options (/itemlock works too)
/ilf debugShow what the addon does at vendors, for bug reports

In Esc > Options > AddOns > ItemLockForever:

  • The click that locks items: Alt + Left Click (default), Alt + Right Click, Ctrl + Alt + Right Click, Ctrl + Right Click, or off
  • Hold to unlock
  • A key binding that locks or unlocks the item under the mouse (also under Key Bindings)
  • The lock, unlock and error sounds, or none
  • The padlock's corner and size

Good to know

  • While a vendor window is open, or Disenchant waits for a target, locked items can't be used, equipped or moved either. Unlock an item (or leave the vendor) to use it.
  • The buy-back can only reach the vendor's buyback list, which holds your last 12 sales.
  • Locks protect against selling, destroying and disenchanting. Mailing and trading still work, and addons that delete items themselves (without the game's destroy dialog) aren't stopped.
  • At the Auction House the click is left to Auctionator, which uses Alt+Click to pick items to post. The key binding still works there.

Privacy

Your locks and settings are stored on your computer, in WoW's saved variables. ItemLockForever sends nothing anywhere. /ilf debug prints only item IDs and counts, so it's safe to share.
